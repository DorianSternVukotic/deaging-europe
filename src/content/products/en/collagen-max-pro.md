caveats:
  - title: Not vegan
    body: This is animal collagen, bovine or fish. Vegan "collagen boosters" supply amino acids and cofactors instead; they are not the same thing.
  - title: Weeks, not days
    body: In the peptide trials hydration moved at 8 weeks and collagen structure from week 4 to 12. The ceramide trial reports changes at 15 days, but it is one small study. Budget for two tubs before you judge it.
  - title: It does not replace sunscreen or a retinoid
    body: Daily SPF prevents most collagen loss in the first place, and a topical retinoid builds collagen at the surface. A supplement adds to that routine; it does not stand in for it.
  - title: Most of the numbers are maker-funded
    body: Rousselot co-authored the Peptan trial and Seppic ran the ceramide study. That is normal for ingredient research, but it is a reason to read the effect sizes as a best case.
  - title: Three amounts are not published online
    body: Aeterna names all four actives and their suppliers but only publishes the collagen dose. The rest are on the tub label, which we will transcribe.

faq:
  - q: When and how do I take it?
    a: >-
      One scoop in 200 ml of cold water, any time of day, every day. The maker suggests cold water because the powder dissolves cleanly in it; it works in coffee or a smoothie too.
  - q: Can I take more than one scoop a day?
    a: >-
      The skin trials used 10 g, which is one scoop. Joint and tendon studies have used 10 to 20 g, so a second scoop is within studied ranges, but there is no evidence it does more for skin.
  - q: What does it taste like?
    a: >-
      The neutral tub is unflavoured and faintly milky. White raspberry and the marine lemon version use natural flavours and a natural sweetener; all three are sugar-free.
  - q: Bovine or marine?
    a: >-
      Same Peptan peptide chemistry, different animal. Choose marine if you avoid beef or want the lemon flavour. No trial shows one beating the other for skin.
  - q: Is it safe?
    a: >-
      Collagen peptides are well tolerated across multi-month trials. Check the allergens: fish in the Marin C+, and the ceramides are wheat-derived although the product is labelled gluten-free. If you have a history of kidney stones, or are pregnant or breastfeeding, ask your doctor first.
  - q: When will I see results?
    a: >-
      The trials measured hydration at 8 weeks and collagen structure from week 4. Plan on two to three tubs before deciding.
